FAQ

Is Shift really free?

Yes. The launcher and all of its core features are free, with no ads. The Supporter Pack only adds optional extras.

How do I activate the Supporter Pack?

After checkout you receive a license key by email. In Shift, open Settings > Info > Supporter Pack, paste the key and press Activate.

Can I use one license on several devices?

No. Each license key is active on one device at a time. You can move it to a different device whenever you like (see below).

Can I move my license to another device?

Yes. Use "Deactivate this device" in the Supporter Pack page to free the slot, then activate the key on the new device. If the old device is lost or was reset before you could deactivate it, open an issue on GitHub with your order number and we will free the slot for you.

I installed Shift from the Microsoft Store. How do I get the Supporter Pack?

The Microsoft Store version sells the Supporter Pack as an in-app add-on: open Settings > Info > Supporter Pack and press Buy. The purchase is tied to your Microsoft account, so it unlocks on every PC where you sign in to the Microsoft Store with the same account. License keys bought on this website are for the installer version from GitHub Releases.

Which devices does Shift support?

Any Windows 10 or 11 PC. It is designed for handheld gaming PCs such as MSI Claw, ROG Ally and Legion Go, and works with any XInput controller.

Can I get a refund?

Shift is free, so you can try everything before you buy, and Supporter Pack sales are final. We do refund a license key that has never been activated, a duplicate purchase, or a key that cannot be activated because of a problem on our side. See the Refund Policy.

How do I get help?

Open an issue on GitHub. For order and payment questions you can also contact Lemon Squeezy support through the link in your receipt email.
