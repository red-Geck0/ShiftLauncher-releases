Refund Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

Shift itself is free, so you can try every core feature on your own device before you decide to buy the Supporter Pack. Because of that, all Supporter Pack sales are final and we do not offer refunds, except in the cases below.

When we do refund

  • The license key was never activated. If you bought a key and have not activated it on any device yet, you can request a full refund. We check the activation status on our side before approving it.
  • Duplicate purchase. If you were charged more than once for the same product by mistake, we refund the extra order.
  • The key cannot be activated because of a problem on our side, and we are unable to fix it for you.

Once a key has been activated on a device, the purchase is final. Moving a license to a new device is always free: see the FAQ.

How to request a refund

  • Use the link in your Lemon Squeezy receipt email, or visit app.lemonsqueezy.com/my-orders to contact Lemon Squeezy about your order, or
  • Open an issue on GitHub with your order number. Do not post your license key or email address publicly.

Refunds are processed by Lemon Squeezy, our Merchant of Record, back to the original payment method. It can take 5 to 10 business days for the refund to appear, depending on your bank.

After a refund

When a refund is issued, the license key is disabled and Supporter features are locked again. Shift itself keeps working as the free version, and none of your games, tags or settings are removed.

Other notes

Refunds are not available for licenses revoked due to abuse or public sharing of the key. As our Merchant of Record, Lemon Squeezy may also decide on refund requests at its own discretion in line with its buyer terms and applicable consumer law. Nothing in this policy limits rights you have under the law of your country.
