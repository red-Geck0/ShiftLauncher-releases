Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

Shift is designed to keep your data on your own PC. We do not run user accounts, we do not include analytics, telemetry or advertising, and we do not sell or share personal data.

1. Data stored on your device

Shift stores its settings, game list cache, tags, artwork choices, playtime records and logs locally in your Windows user profile. This data never leaves your PC unless you choose to send it, for example by attaching a log file to a bug report.

2. Store accounts

If you choose to sign in to a store (for example Xbox, Epic Games or GOG) to see games you own but have not installed, Shift talks directly to that store's official servers. Sign-in happens on the store's own login page. The resulting access tokens are stored only on your PC and are never sent to us.

3. Online services Shift contacts

To show artwork, catalogs and prices, Shift requests public data from services such as Steam, Epic Games, GOG, Microsoft / Xbox, SteamGridDB and GitHub. Like any web request, these services can see your IP address. Their own privacy policies apply to that data. Shift can also download optional mod tools from their official sources when you ask it to.

4. Purchases and license keys

Supporter Pack purchases are processed by Lemon Squeezy, our Merchant of Record. Lemon Squeezy collects the information needed to complete the order, such as your name, email address, billing country and payment details, under the Lemon Squeezy Privacy Policy. We receive order details (name, email, country, product and amount) so we can provide support. We never see your full card details.

When you activate, validate or deactivate a license key, Shift sends only the key (and, when deactivating, the activation ID Lemon Squeezy issued for it) to the Lemon Squeezy License API. Nothing about your PC, your hardware or your games is sent.

5. Support requests

If you contact us through GitHub Issues, anything you post there is public and subject to GitHub's privacy policy. Please do not post license keys or personal information in a public issue.

6. Your rights

You can delete all data Shift stores locally by uninstalling the app and removing its folder in %LOCALAPPDATA%. For questions about order data held by Lemon Squeezy, or to request its deletion, contact us or Lemon Squeezy support.

7. Children

Shift is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal data.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

9. Contact

Privacy questions can be sent through GitHub Issues.
